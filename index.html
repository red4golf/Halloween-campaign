How it works

Your steps are the mission.

This runs on The Tryout™, a guided walking-adventure app built on the A Step Ahead™ platform. The premise: the zombie apocalypse has hit, and a crew of survivors is testing whether you've got what it takes to join the Vanguards. Your daily steps — tracked from your phone, no wearable required — move your crew through the story over two weeks.

Why it matters

Every crew that finishes helps AWT fly again.

Angel's Wings Transport is an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Volunteer pilots fly their own aircraft, on their own dime, for people who have no other way to get where they need to go.

Wounded warriors

Veterans & military families

Free flights for wounded warriors and military families getting to care, ceremony, or home.

Rescue & relief

Animals escaping danger

Free transport for rescue animals moving toward foster, adoption, or medical care.

Patients in need

Medical patients

Free flights for patients who can't afford or manage the trip to the care they need.

Teams

Five lanterns to light.

Five founding team-leader registrations are sponsored free by AWT. Each approved leader can invite up to nine paid teammates, building a crew of up to ten — though a crew of just the captain still counts toward launching the event.

  • Leader places are limited and approved by AWT — apply on the Teams page.
  • Once approved, you get a tracked invite link and a recruiting script to fill your crew.
  • Only confirmed leaders and organizations appear publicly.
