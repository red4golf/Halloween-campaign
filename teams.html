Team structure

How a crew comes together.

Five founding team-leader registrations are sponsored free by AWT. Each approved leader can invite up to nine paid teammates, building a crew of up to ten — that's AWT's cap per team, not a requirement. A crew can be just one committed captain. What actually launches the mission is AWT reaching ten total registered walkers across the whole event, spread across as few as two crews or as many as ten.

  • Leader places are limited and approved by AWT.
  • Once approved, you get a tracked invite link, a launch message, a 72-hour reminder, and a final-call template.
  • Only confirmed leaders and organizations appear publicly.
  • We ask leaders to personally invite 15–20 people and fill as many of the nine open spots as they can — though a crew of one still counts.

Field Manual · Leader Recruiting Script

The pitch (text, email, or in person)

"I'm leading a team for Angel's Wings Transport's Halloween walking challenge — Mission: On The Move. It's $7, two weeks, totally phone-based (The Tryout™ app turns your daily steps into a zombie-survival story), and it helps keep AWT's volunteer pilots flying free for veterans, rescue animals, and medical patients. I've got a spot on my crew with your name on it — want in?"

If they ask "what do I actually do?"

"Walk like you normally would. The app tracks steps from your phone — no fitness tracker needed — and turns them into a story you're playing through with our crew over two weeks, Oct 17–31. Light difficulty, all fitness levels."

If they hesitate on price

"The $7 covers your app access — AWT's actual cost is $5 — and the rest supports AWT's flights directly. You'll get a receipt."
Field Manual · Recruiting Timeline

The same cadence every leader gets, so nobody's guessing when to reach out.

  • T−14 days Leaders approved; each gets a tracked link, launch email, social post, and this script.
  • T−10 days Send personal invitations to 15–20 warm contacts, aiming for nine paid teammates.
  • T−7 days AWT announces the campaign publicly through email and social.
  • T−3 days Share real team progress — who's in, how close you are to full.
  • Final 48h Last-call reminder, only if seats genuinely remain.
  • Days 1, 7, 13 Encourage your crew — AWT shares mission updates in parallel.
  • After Oct 31 An impact recap, plus a separate invitation to keep supporting AWT.
