Team structure

How a crew comes together.

Five founding team-leader registrations are sponsored free by AWT. Each approved leader can invite up to nine paid teammates, building a crew of up to ten. That's AWT's cap per team, not a requirement; a crew can be just one committed captain. What actually launches the mission is AWT reaching ten total registered walkers across the whole event, spread across as few as two crews or as many as ten.

  • Leader places are limited and approved by AWT.
  • Once approved, you get a tracked invite link, a launch message, a 72-hour reminder, and a final-call template.
  • Only confirmed leaders and organizations appear publicly.
  • We ask leaders to personally invite 15–20 people and fill as many of the nine open spots as they can, though a crew of one still counts.
