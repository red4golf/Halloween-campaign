The Tryout™ · A Step Ahead™ Challenge

Can you make it out?

The army has announced a total evacuation — the city's overrun. Your daily steps are how your crew moves toward the bunker outside town over two weeks. Here's exactly what that means on your phone.

The premise

You're trying out for the Vanguards.

A remaining group of survivors is being trained by soldiers to move and survive in a world overrun by zombies. Passing The Tryout™ is how you earn your place among the citizen Vanguards. It's a light, campy story layer — family-friendly, all ages — built on top of an ordinary walking goal.

FIELD NOTE: The Tryout™ is a real product from The Outbreak Store (A Step Ahead™, published by FIX Health). AWT purchases mission tickets and runs this challenge independently — we are not affiliated with or endorsed by FIX Health.
The mechanics

Your steps move the mission.

DURATION

2 weeks

Oct 17–31, 2026. The mission runs on a fixed daily/weekly clock.

PACE

Light difficulty

Standard prescription: roughly 6,000–7,000 steps a day and 30–45 exercise minutes a week.

TRACKING

Phone only, no tracker required

Syncs from your phone's built-in pedometer, or Apple Health / Google Fit if you already use a Fitbit or similar.

CREW SIZE

Up to 10 per crew

Ten is AWT's cap per crew, not a requirement — a crew of one committed captain works too. The Tryout™ needs 10 total participants to launch a mission, and AWT combines everyone's crews across the whole event to reach that.

Every walker's steps count toward the crew's shared progress through the story — this isn't a solo stopwatch app. All fitness levels are welcome; the standard prescription starts easy and eases you toward the goal week over week.

What you need

Requirements & setup.

Phone

iPhone 6 or newer (iOS 11+), or Android 5.1.1+ with at least 2GB RAM. The app doesn't run on iPad — bring a phone.

Fitness tracker

Optional. Apple Health and Google Fit both pull data from most trackers automatically — or skip a tracker entirely and let your phone count steps.

FIELD NOTE: Kids playing along with a family crew will need a phone, not just a tablet — worth knowing before you recruit a family team.
