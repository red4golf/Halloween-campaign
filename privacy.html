Privacy

What we collect, and why.

This page covers the current pre-registration phase of Mission: On The Move. It will be expanded before paid registration opens to cover payment data specifically.

Draft — last reviewed with today's site update. Not yet linked from a cookie/consent banner because this site sets no tracking cookies.

What this page covers

This notice describes the data Angel's Wings Transport ("AWT," "we," "us") collects through the Mission: On The Move pages at this URL, during the current pre-registration phase: leader applications and "get launch updates" signups. It does not yet cover payment processing, because paid registration is not open (see Terms & Refunds).

What we collect

  • Leader applications (Teams & Leaders page): name, email address, organization (if given), and anything you write in the "who's on your crew" field.
  • Launch-update signups (home page): email address, and whether you opted in to occasional AWT news.
  • Technical metadata attached to a submission: the page you submitted from and the time you submitted it, so we can tell forms apart and troubleshoot.

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or third-party ad trackers on this site.

How we use it

  • To respond to leader applications and confirm approved spots.
  • To send launch updates and registration instructions once registration opens, to anyone who applied or signed up.
  • To send optional AWT news only to people who separately checked that consent box — never bundled with the required operational messages above.

Who it's shared with

Form submissions are processed through Make.com, an automation platform that routes your submission into AWT's internal records. Make acts as a data processor on AWT's behalf and does not use your information for its own purposes.

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it with A Step Ahead™ / FIX Health at this stage — that only happens later, and only for people who actually register and pay, so the app provider can issue an access code. That exchange will be described here before payment opens.

How long we keep it

Leader-application and signup records are kept for the life of this campaign and a reasonable period after, for AWT's own recordkeeping and to follow up on the mission's impact. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see below.

Your choices

  • The marketing-news checkbox is always optional and unchecked by default. You can opt out at any time.
  • Operational messages tied to a leader application or registration (confirmations, launch updates you specifically requested) aren't part of that optional consent, since you asked for them directly.
  • Email charles@awtrescue.org to see, correct, or delete what we have on file.

Before payment opens

This notice will be updated to name the actual payment processor, describe how a completed payment connects to an access code, and cover the app provider's own data collection once registration is live — consistent with AWT's own launch checklist for this campaign.

Questions
