What your $7 covers.

No hidden math. Here's exactly where the money goes, and the questions AWT hears most before someone joins.

$7 to join · one walker
  • Challenge-app access: $5, AWT's current cost for one A Step Ahead™ Tryout™ ticket, purchased through The Outbreak Store (FIX Health's storefront).
  • Remaining $2 supports AWT's event administration and overhead, after applicable processing and event costs. It never pays a pilot or funds one specific flight.

Before you join

Questions answered.

Will I pay Angel's Wings Transport or the app company?

You register and pay AWT directly. To play, you'll create or use a separate account with A Step Ahead™ (the app provider) and accept its own terms and privacy policy; that account and AWT's registration are separate.

Do I need a fitness tracker or wearable?

No. The Tryout™ works from your phone's built-in step counter, or syncs from Apple Health, Google Fit, or a compatible wearable if you already have one. All fitness levels are welcome — the standard prescription is roughly 6,000–7,000 steps a day.

What phone do I need?

iPhone 6 or newer (iOS 11+), or Android 5.1.1+ with at least 2GB RAM. The app does not run on iPad — kids playing along will need a phone.

How big is a team, and why?

Up to ten; that's AWT's cap per crew, not a requirement. A crew can be just one committed captain. AWT set a minimum of ten total registered walkers across the whole event to launch the mission, combining everyone's crews to reach it. Five founding leader spots are sponsored by AWT; each leader can recruit up to nine paid teammates to build out their crew.

Will I be added to AWT's newsletter?

AWT will send the registration, payment, code, and event-support messages needed to run the challenge regardless of marketing consent. Future AWT news is optional and requires a separate, unchecked opt-in — see the signup on the home page or the leader application.

What's the refund policy?

The final policy will be published before payment opens, including the cancellation deadline and what happens if AWT can't deliver a working access code. See Terms & Refunds for the current structure.

Is this appropriate for kids or a nervous walker?

Yes — A Step Ahead™'s tone and visuals are family-friendly for all ages. "Zombie survival" here means a light, campy story layer over an ordinary walking goal, not anything graphic.

Is AWT actually a registered nonprofit?

Yes. Angel's Wings Transport has held federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status since August 2015 (EIN 47-4874470) and is a registered Washington State charity, serving WA, OR, CA, ID, and MT.
