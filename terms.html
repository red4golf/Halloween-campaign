Terms & refunds

The structure, ahead of the final policy.

Registration isn't open yet, so the final refund policy isn't either. This page lays out the framework AWT is committing to, and what still needs a real number filled in.

Draft framework. Final refund policy publishes before payment opens.

Registration

Registration is $7 per walker, paid directly to Angel's Wings Transport (AWT), not to FIX Health or A Step Ahead™. Your $7 is not a donation in the tax-deductible sense; see Pricing & FAQ for the full breakdown, and consult your own tax adviser regarding any deduction.

Registering reserves your place on a crew of up to ten. Sponsored leader seats are limited, non-transferable, and have no cash value.

What you get

A working access code for The Tryout™ mission on the A Step Ahead™ app, tied to your crew and the Oct 17–31, 2026 event window. You'll separately accept A Step Ahead™'s own terms and privacy policy when you create an account with them; that's between you and FIX Health, not AWT.

Refunds – framework

AWT is committing to a policy that will, at minimum:

  • State a clear cancellation deadline before a code is issued, after which the registration fee is no longer refundable.
  • Provide a refund if AWT fails to deliver a working access code due to an error on AWT's end, subject to the final terms published before payment opens.
  • Explain what happens if a leader's crew doesn't reach AWT's event-wide minimum of ten registered walkers needed to launch the mission.

The exact cancellation deadline and process will be published here before payment opens, not assumed and not implied by anything elsewhere on this site.

Assumption of risk

Mission: On The Move is a self-paced walking activity. By registering, you confirm you're physically able to participate and agree to walk at your own pace and within your own limits. AWT is not liable for injury, loss, or harm arising from your participation, to the extent allowed by law. If you have a health condition that could make walking unsafe, check with a doctor before joining.

Participants under 18 need a parent or guardian to register on their behalf and to agree to these terms for them.

Leader responsibilities

Approved leaders agree to recruit in good faith toward their crew of nine paid teammates, and to represent AWT's mission honestly using the materials provided. AWT can withdraw an unfilled or inactive leader spot to offer it to someone else, with notice.

Conduct

This is a family-friendly community challenge. AWT can remove a participant or leader for harassment, fraud, or misuse of the app or this site, subject to the refund framework above.

Independence from FIX Health

This campaign is independently organized by AWT. The Tryout™ and A Step Ahead™ are trademarks of FIX Health. AWT purchases and administers mission tickets on participants' behalf and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FIX Health. Any dispute about the app itself is between you and FIX Health under their own terms.

Changes

AWT may update these terms before registration opens as the remaining launch details (final refund deadline, vendor confirmation) are settled. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
