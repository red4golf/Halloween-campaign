Why Angel's Wings Transport exists

One soldier's homecoming, with no one there to meet him.

AWT's founder, a retired member of the military, was present for an Honorable Transfer of Remains in Spokane, Washington — a soldier's body coming home. The soldier's parents weren't there. They couldn't afford the gas money to make the drive. The only people at that homecoming were airline passengers, the flight crew, and the funeral honors team. That's the moment AWT exists to make sure doesn't happen again.

The mission

Three reasons AWT's pilots fly for free.

Angel's Wings Transport is an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Its pilots fly their own general aviation aircraft — single or twin engine, often small enough to land at airstrips a commercial airline never would — at their own expense, for people who have no other way to get where they need to go.

Wounded warriors

Veterans & military families

So no wounded warrior, veteran, or family member is denied access to medical care, a service animal, or their soldier's bedside or funeral because of distance or travel cost. AWT serves every era, from WWII veterans to the newest recruit — deployment not required.

Rescue & relief

Animals escaping danger

Transport from kill shelters toward foster, rescue, and adoption — 5 to 10 animals per flight, riding in the cabin in secured, climate-controlled carriers. There's never a fee to the sending or receiving shelter.

Patients in need

Medical patients

No- or low-cost flights for ambulatory patients with financial need who can't otherwise make the trip to the care they require.

Scale of the need

This isn't a small gap.

Roughly 10,000 military funerals happen nationwide every month. When AWT's founder looked for existing support for a family in that situation, there wasn't any — beyond what a funeral director could pull from their own pocket. That gap is what Angel's Wings Transport was built to close, one flight at a time.

FIELD NOTE: AWT is a registered Washington State charity and has held federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status since August 2015 (EIN 47-4874470), serving WA, OR, CA, ID, and MT.
How flights happen

Donated aircraft, donated seats.

Missions fly one of two ways: a volunteer pilot donates a general-aviation flight in their own aircraft, or a commercial airline ticket is donated, purchased by AWT, or covered by an airline employee's donated flight voucher. Every flight is weighed against safety first — weather, aircraft condition, and pilot readiness can and do cancel a mission before it launches.

Angel's Wings Transport runs on donations and grants — and on challenges exactly like this one.
